# ADR 013: Reversal wherever feasible; explicit warning and acknowledgment for irreversible effects

Status: accepted in approved specification, 2026-10-19.

## Contents

- [Decision](#decision)
- [Benefit and accepted tradeoff](#benefit-and-accepted-tradeoff)

## Decision

Reversal wherever feasible; explicit warning and acknowledgment for irreversible effects.

## Benefit and accepted tradeoff

Supports recovery without promising that all side effects can be undone.
